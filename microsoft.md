# Microsoft Benchmark Summary

Microsoft is a multinational technology company headquartered in Redmond, Washington. The company operates across software, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, enterprise productivity, and gaming.

Key business segments include:
- Intelligent Cloud (Azure)
- Productivity and Business Processes
- More Personal Computing

Microsoft has demonstrated strong recurring revenue growth through enterprise subscriptions and cloud infrastructure services. Azure remains one of the leading global cloud platforms alongside AWS and Google Cloud.

The company continues investing heavily in artificial intelligence, including partnerships with OpenAI and integration of AI capabilities across Microsoft products such as Copilot.
